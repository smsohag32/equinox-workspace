# Privacy Policy for Equinox Workspace

**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use Equinox Workspace ("Extension", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Equinox Workspace is designed with privacy as a priority. We collect and process the absolute minimum amount of information required for the Extension to function properly.

- **Local Storage Data:** Your tasks, customized settings, theme preferences, and uploaded background images are stored entirely locally on your device using your browser's local storage capabilities (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Google Account Information:** If you choose to use features that require Google authentication (such as syncing tasks with Google Keep), the Extension requests access to your email address and profile info via Google's official Identity API. This data is only used locally for authentication and API requests. We do not store this information on any external servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **To provide the service:** Your local data is used strictly to display your personalized dashboard (clock, tasks, background, timer).
- **No external transmission:** We do **not** transmit, collect, or store your personal data, tasks, or settings on any external servers, databases, or third-party tracking services. Everything stays in your browser.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Extension may interact with the following third-party services directly from your browser to provide specific features:
- **Google APIs:** For authentication and fetching Google Search suggestions.
- **ZenQuotes API:** To fetch daily motivational quotes.
- **NASA / Wikimedia APIs:** To fetch background images.

These services may receive your IP address as part of standard web requests, governed by their respective Privacy Policies.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or transfer your personal data, browser history, or any other information to any third parties. 

## 5. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally in your browser, you have complete control over it. You can delete all your data at any time by:
- Uninstalling the Extension.
- Clearing your browser's extension storage.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support section on the Chrome Web Store or through the GitHub repository.
